Games stop at 2,000 ticks unless the Agent column says otherwise. The first two agents played 8 games per map (2 on the large maps), so their numbers can be a few points off either way.

What the latest round of training changed. NoWhereToRun9x8 and letMeOut now have their own specialist agents, trained from agent_16x16_v3.pt on just that map, and they took those maps from 0% and 41% to 66% and 97%. agent_24x24_v2.pt trained another 97M steps on the 24×24 maps with player 0's board turned 180° (so the network sees every game from the same corner) and older agents kept in its opponent pool. It went from 53% to 59% on basesWorkers24x24, and from its weaker corner (player_0) from 3 to 7 wins in 16 games. It still never beats barricades24x24, and it fell from 41% to 12% on the 32×32 map it never trained on, so that row keeps the earlier agent_24x24.pt. A 16×16 run with the same changes ended slightly worse than agent_16x16_v3.pt (93% against 96% across the twelve basesWorkers16x16 maps), so it was not kept.

To reproduce a row: python training/play.py training/agents/<agent>.pt --map <map> --opponent mixed --games 32, adding --max-steps 5000 on the 24×24 and 32×32 maps and --max-steps 3000 for the two specialists. Agents trained with the board turn apply it on their own when they play.
